EMERGENCY SAFETY GUIDE

What To Do Before We Arrive

Taking a few simple precautions during a plumbing emergency can help reduce water damage and keep everyone safe until professional help arrives.

Turn Off the Water Supply

If it is safe to do so, close the main water valve to stop or reduce the flow of water.

Stay Away From Electrical Areas

Keep away from wet electrical outlets, appliances and exposed wires. Never touch electrical equipment in standing water.

Protect Your Belongings

If safe, move furniture, electronics and valuables away from the affected area.

Contact Our Emergency Team

Explain the problem clearly and share the location of the plumbing issue so our team can prepare.

Important: If water is near electrical equipment, do not enter the area. Contact emergency services or a qualified electrician when necessary.
