PREVENT PROBLEMS BEFORE THEY START

Simple Plumbing Habits, Lasting Protection

Regular plumbing maintenance helps prevent unexpected breakdowns, costly repairs, and water wastage. Follow these simple tips to keep your plumbing system working efficiently.

Check for Water Leaks

Inspect taps, pipes, and connections regularly for dripping water.

Keep Drains Clean

Remove visible debris and avoid putting grease or food waste into drains.

Maintain Water Tanks

Arrange periodic water tank inspections and cleaning.

Monitor Water Pressure

Pay attention to sudden changes in water flow or pressure.

Inspect Bathroom Fixtures

Look for loose fittings, corrosion, and dripping taps.
